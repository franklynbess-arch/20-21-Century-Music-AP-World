20th & 21st Century Music

From jazz and rock to hip-hop and pop, music from 1900 to today has shaped culture, politics, identity, and globalization across the world.

Origins Change Over Time Artists Impact Historical Thinking Skills

Origins → Where/How Did It Start?

Modern music began changing rapidly in the early 1900s because of industrialization, urbanization, migration, and new technology like radios and record players.


Jazz started in New Orleans by blending African rhythms, blues, and European musical traditions. Later genres like rock, pop, electronic music, and hip-hop developed from earlier musical styles.


Music spread internationally through migration, colonial connections, mass media, and eventually the internet.

Change Over Time → How Has It Evolved?

1. The Rise of Jazz (1920s)

Jazz became one of the first globally influential genres. It represented creativity and cultural innovation during the Harlem Renaissance.

2. Rock & Roll Explosion (1950s–1960s)

Rock music changed youth culture around the world. Artists like Elvis Presley and The Beatles influenced fashion, rebellion, and social movements.

3. Hip-Hop & Digital Streaming (1980s–Present)

Hip-hop began in New York City and became a global movement. Streaming platforms like Spotify and YouTube later transformed how music is created and shared worldwide.

Examples of Artists & Genres

Louis Armstrong — Jazz

Louis Armstrong was one of the most influential jazz musicians of the 20th century. His trumpet playing and singing helped make jazz internationally popular.

Elvis Presley — Rock & Roll

Known as the “King of Rock and Roll,” Elvis Presley helped popularize rock music in the 1950s and changed modern pop culture.

Michael Jackson — Pop

Michael Jackson became one of the biggest global entertainers ever. His music videos, dancing, and performances revolutionized pop music.

Tupac Shakur — Hip-Hop

Tupac used rap music to discuss social inequality, violence, identity, and life in urban America. He became one of hip-hop’s most important voices.

BTS — K-Pop

BTS helped make Korean pop music globally successful. Their worldwide fanbase shows how music spreads through globalization and social media.

Taylor Swift — Modern Pop

Taylor Swift represents modern music in the streaming era. She is known for songwriting, global tours, and influence on digital music culture.

Impact → Why Does It Matter?

Globally

Music connects people across cultures and languages. Genres like jazz, reggae, rock, hip-hop, and K-pop became global through migration, technology, and the internet.

Personally

Music affects emotions, memories, and identity. People use music for motivation, celebration, protest, and self-expression.

How Would You Convince Someone It Matters?

Music reflects history and social change. Protest songs supported civil rights movements, anti-war movements, and political activism. Music also helps preserve culture and traditions.

Historical Thinking Skills

Cause & Effect

New technologies like radios, CDs, streaming apps, and social media changed how music spreads. As a result, artists can now reach global audiences instantly.

Comparison

Jazz in the 1920s and hip-hop today are similar because both started as cultural expressions from marginalized communities and later became globally influential genres.

AP World Connection

Music connects to AP World themes like globalization, cultural diffusion, industrialization, technology, and identity. Music spread globally through trade, migration, colonization, and the internet.
